# Handoff: Oct 27 2026 (controller fix + layouts, special move buffer)

**Build first:** C# changed (engine). Open the project in Godot and press **Build** (hammer, top right) before playing.
These engine changes were **not compiled or play-tested** before handing off (no .NET in the work environment): if the
Build shows an error, the files to look at are the three below.

## 1. D-pad / hitbox Down didn't work in fights (playtester)
Cause: in Options > Button Settings, opening a button row and pressing D-DOWN gave that button D-DOWN and *removed* it from
Down (only the left stick was left), and that was saved. Fixed: rebinds always swap; directions are on the page; old saves
get their D-pad + stick directions back on load. Full write-up: `claude/options.md` → Rebinding.

**Three controller layouts:** Button Settings has a **Layout** row per controller: Controller / Arcade Stick /
Hitbox-Snack Box. Every row, directions too, can be any button, D-pad direction, stick direction or trigger. Tables:
`claude/options.md`, `docs/controls.md`. Files: `game/autoloads/controls.gd`, `scenes/options/options_menu.gd`
(GDScript only). Backups: `_backup_stage_select/pre_pad_layouts_oct27/`.

## 2. Special move buffer (all fighters)
Before, a special / EX / super came out only if its button was pressed on a frame where it was already allowed. Done a
little early it was simply dropped: during a normal's recovery, a frame before the normal hit, in blockstun, while landing.

Now:
- **Buffer, 8 frames** (`StanceManager.NetsuSpecialBufferFrames`): a finished special input (2+ directions, then a button)
  that can't come out yet is kept for 8 frames and comes out on the first frame it can: from neutral when recovery /
  stun ends, or as a cancel the moment the normal hits or is blocked. Frames frozen in hitstop don't count, so it can be
  pressed right before the hit lands. A whiffed normal still can't be special-cancelled; its special comes out after it
  only if the recovery ends inside those 8 frames.
- Not kept: a move you lack the meter for, or one for the wrong surface (an air special pressed on the ground). Plain
  buttons and normals are unchanged (normals keep the 4-frame stun buffer).
- A newer special input replaces an older one; doing any move clears it.
- **Motions a bit looser** (`InputManager.cs`, numbers at the top): up to 8 frames between two directions (was 6), a
  direction held up to 14 (was 12), the button up to 8 frames after the last direction (was 6), the whole motion window
  14 + 8 per direction (was 10 + 8). This supersedes the numbers in `claude/game-systems.md` → Input leniency.
- Rollback-safe: the kept move and its frames are saved with the match (`StanceManagerState` keys 4-5).

Files: `engine/sakuga/Components/StanceManager.cs`, `InputManager.cs`, `engine/sakuga/GameState/StanceManagerState.cs`.
Backups: `_backup_stage_select/pre_special_buffer_oct27/` (`.bak`).

### Tuning
Too lenient (specials coming out when you meant a normal)? Lower `NetsuSpecialBufferFrames` (8) first, then
`NetsuLatePress` / `NetsuMotionGap`. Too strict? Raise them. One number each, then Build.

## Testing checklist
1. **Build.**
2. Controller / hitbox: Options > Button Settings: Down shows D-DOWN. Training: crouch with the d-pad. Switch Layout
   to Arcade Stick / Hitbox and check the buttons in the table.
3. Buffer: Training, 2B and input 236A *during* 2B's startup (before it hits): it cancels on hit. Block a long string
   and input 236A / 623A in the last part of blockstun: comes out the first frame you're free. Whiff 5A, input 236A
   during its recovery: comes out right after (only if inside 8 frames).
4. Make sure normals still come out normally after walking / crouching (no accidental 236A on a crouch → walk → A).
5. Online: a quick match to confirm nothing desyncs (the new state is saved, but it wasn't sync-tested).
