Oct 17, 2026 · @Bloo

Read it as where each fighter wants the round to be played; positions are our proposal, not measured data.

Gap: no one is a defensive close-range character (grappler or counter-fighter). Silica's command grab partly fills it; a future slot could be a true grappler.

## Character sheets

Each sheet gives the gameplan in one line, how the character wins a round, the signature mechanic, starter tools and the weakness that makes them fair. Reference DNA is the feel we borrow, not a copy of the reference's kit.

### Cecilia Richter — Close range rushdown

DNA: Narmaya's stance flow; Rean's Eight Leaves forms. Campaign: level 14 Eight Leaves One Blade Disciple (custom Fighter), a tachi infused with lightning, slim build (like Alisa). Light-hearted until the fight starts, then calm and precise. Profile ratings: Damage A, Defense C, Support C, difficulty 3/5.

- **Gameplan:** Precision rushdown. A fast run (Second Form, Gale) and long tachi normals get her in. She doesn't mash; she makes a few clean cuts, and each Craft that lands advances her Moon Phase.
- **Win condition:** Land an opening, carry to the corner with 214C Thunder Strike and build Moon Phase. At True Morning Moon, Raijin cashes the gauge in for a huge hit. Blockers get opened by Raijin (overhead), lows and throws. Mashers run into Defensive Stance counters, and jumpers into the invincible 623C Morning Moon.
- **Signature mechanic: Moon Phase.** A 5-step gauge: New, Crescent, Half, Morning, True Morning. It goes up 1 for each Craft that hits and each successful counter, and resets to New Moon after Raijin cashes it in. Phases power up her Crafts the way her cards do: New Moon = stronger Opening Edge, Half Moon = Fujin hits more times, Morning Moon = counters do more damage and hit both sides, True Morning Moon = Raijin +40% damage, then back to New Moon.
- **Defensive Stance (Fifth Form) is her counter stance:** she sheathes and catches the next strike. A whiff is punishable. Passive from her profile: she starts every round with one Counter charge, so her first counter of the round is faster.
- **Weakness:** Low defense (more damage taken). Defensive Stance and Focused Mind are reads that lose badly to a throw or a wait. Swordwind Slash is slow, so real zoners still outshoot her.

### Flan — Close range rushdown (brawler)

DNA: Makoto's charged punches and Yang's "gets stronger when hurt".

- **Gameplan:** Slow, steady walk forward behind armored and charged punches. She doesn't dash in; she makes the opponent feel trapped.
- **Win condition:** Corner the opponent, then threaten charged punches (guard crush) and throws. Late in the round, Heat turns 2 hits into a full combo.
- **Signature mechanic: Charge + Heat.** Holding C charges a punch through 3 levels (level 3 = armor + guard crush). A Heat gauge fills when she takes or blocks damage, raising her damage and armor while full.
- **Tools:** 236C charge straight, 214B parry stance, 623B rising uppercut, 41236C command grab-style body blow, super = max-Heat rush.
- **Weakness:** Slow movement and weak air game; zoners and fast fighters (Juel) can avoid her.

### Juel — Glass cannon

DNA: Yosuke's speed buff and ninja tricks; Chipp's teleports and low health.

- **Gameplan:** Run, jump, teleport. Juel never stands still; he picks when the fight happens.
- **Win condition:** Fastest and most confusing mixups in the cast (cross-ups, teleport left/right, instant overheads), with the highest damage per opening.
- **Signature mechanic: Haste.** A short buff (22B, or a meter skill) that speeds his walk, dash and recovery. His best routes need it, so the opponent knows when to fear him.
- **Tools:** Double air dash, wall cling, 214A/B teleport (behind / above), 236A dagger toss, j.2C dive kick.
- **Weakness:** Lowest health; poor reversal; any clean anti-air or a Silica/Flan combo takes most of his life.

### Silica — Bruiser

DNA: Nanase's big, committed normals.

- **Gameplan:** Plant herself at the edge of her heavy buttons and win every trade. Easy to pick up, hard to beat up close.
- **Win condition:** Few hits, big damage. One counter-hit heavy or command grab is a third of a health bar.
- **Signature mechanic: Armored heavies.** Most C normals and specials absorb one hit, so mashing loses to her. Paired with a command grab (63214C) to beat blocking.
- **Tools:** 236C armored charge, 63214C command grab, 214B ground slam (knockdown), 623C anti-air, super = cinematic grab.
- **Weakness:** Slowest walk and dash; armor is beaten by throws, multi-hit moves and lows; zoners keep her out.

### Noah — Space-controlling, hyper-aggressive setplay (high risk / high reward)

DNA: Carmine's blood traps and spending his own life for power.

- **Gameplan:** Fill the space in front of him with traps, then rush through them. He is a trap character who wants to be close, not far.
- **Win condition:** Push the opponent into the corner covered in traps; every block or hit there can detonate for huge damage and long combos.
- **Signature mechanic: Blood debt.** Placing traps costs recoverable (grey) health. Landing hits heals it back; getting hit loses it for good. At low health he can't afford traps, so his power depends on staying ahead.
- **Tools:** 236A/B/C trap at three distances, 214A/B detonate, 623C blood geyser anti-air, 22B pull-in, super = detonate every trap at once.
- **Weakness:** Self-damage; a lost exchange can cost twice. Rushdown that ignores his traps (Flan's armor, Juel's teleports) punishes him hardest.

### Soran — All-rounder with setplay

DNA: Minazuki's dependable all-round kit; Wagner's single-setup pressure.

- **Gameplan:** Good normals, a projectile, a reversal; the honest fighter that teaches the game. Setplay is one extra layer, not the whole plan.
- **Win condition:** Win neutral with solid buttons, get a knockdown, place his sigil and run a mixup the opponent must respect on wake-up.
- **Signature mechanic: One sigil.** 214C places a single sigil; a new one replaces the old. His specials use it: teleport to it, detonate it (hit from behind), or bounce off it to extend combos.
- **Tools:** 236A projectile, 623C reversal, 214C sigil, 214A teleport-to-sigil, super = sigil explosion that also refreshes it.
- **Weakness:** Best at nothing. Specialists beat him in their own range; placing the sigil in neutral costs time.

### Eustace — Mid-range setplay (partner character)

DNA: Ken and Koromaru: one fighter, two bodies.

- **Gameplan:** Send the partner out, then attack from the other side. He works at the range where both bodies threaten at once.
- **Win condition:** Pincer the opponent: partner from behind or low while Eustace hits high, so blocking one side opens the other. Long blockstrings with the partner covering gaps.
- **Signature mechanic: Companion.** Command moves (22A/B/C) send the partner to attack, wait, or return. If the partner is hit it goes on a cooldown, and Eustace is noticeably weaker alone.
- **Tools:** Spear-style long normals, 22A/B/C partner commands, 236B lunge, 623C reversal with partner, super = tandem rush.
- **Weakness:** The partner is a weak point; Lila and Ronald can shoot it away. Most to learn in the cast after Noah.

### Leticia — Hybrid zoner (aggressive)

DNA: Noel's gun-fu chains; Kaguya's mid-range control.

- **Gameplan:** Shoot to make the opponent block or jump, then dash in behind her own shots. Her zoning is the approach.
- **Win condition:** Mid-range shots into dash-canceled pressure; chip and frame advantage until the opponent tries to escape, then a tick throw or anti-air shot.
- **Signature mechanic: Six-shot cylinder.** Shots chain into each other in any order (A/B/C angles) and can be canceled into a dash. After six she must reload (22A, a short animation), the moment opponents wait for.
- **Tools:** 236A/B/C shot (straight / up / low), 214B dash-cancel, 623B point-blank shot (anti-air), 22 reload, super = full-cylinder barrage.
- **Weakness:** Reload windows; weak reversal without meter; Juel and Cecilia get past shots and pressure her.

### Ronald — Hybrid zoner (defensive)

DNA: Eustace (GBVS)'s rifle, charged shots and patient keep-away.

- **Gameplan:** Hold the far-mid range, shoot anyone who walks in, anti-air anyone who jumps. He wants the opponent to make the first move.
- **Win condition:** Take a life lead with chip and punishes, then make the opponent come to him; timeouts are a valid win.
- **Signature mechanic: Charge shots.** Holding B or C charges a shot (faster / harder / guard-breaking at full charge). Releasing early is a quick poke, so he always has an answer.
- **Tools:** 236B/C charge shot, 214A flashbang or trap (escape and setup), 623A anti-air shot, 22B backstep-shot, super = piercing full-screen shot.
- **Weakness:** Bad up close with slow normals; once cornered he has only his escape tool. Cecilia, Juel and Flan's armor are his problems.

### Sara — Long range setplay

DNA: Weiss's glyphs and summons.

- **Gameplan:** Spend the first seconds placing glyphs, then fight inside the zones they cover. She thinks a move ahead of the opponent.
- **Win condition:** Lock the screen with glyphs that fire on a delay, so her own attack and a glyph hit at the same time (high + low, or front + back). Chip and lockdown from full screen.
- **Signature mechanic: Glyphs (max 3).** 214A/B/C places a glyph near / mid / far. Each fires a delayed attack when she presses its button again, or acts as a teleport point. A fourth glyph removes the oldest.
- **Tools:** 214X glyphs, 236A ice projectile, 22X teleport to a glyph, 623C rising glyph (reversal), super = summoned knight slash across the screen.
- **Weakness:** Slow start each round and after knockdowns; if rushed before glyphs are up she is weak. Cecilia and Juel can stop setup.

### Lila — Zoner

DNA: Rise's support persona: scanning, radar, rhythm.

- **Gameplan:** The purest projectile fighter. Every button is about distance; she reacts to what the opponent does instead of setting traps.
- **Win condition:** Hit with projectiles to stack Analysis marks; at 3 marks her projectiles get stronger (more hits, homing). Over a round she snowballs into a screen the opponent can't cross.
- **Signature mechanic: Analysis.** Hits add marks on the opponent that last until the round ends or she is hit hard (combo of 3+ hits wipes them). That makes every clean hit on her a reset of her plan.
- **Tools:** 236A/B/C drones at three heights, 214B radar pulse (reveals and slows), 623C push-away reversal, j.236B air projectile, super = rhythm barrage (timed presses add damage).
- **Weakness:** Lowest walk speed and worst up close; few combos without marks. Juel, Cecilia and Leticia can close the gap.

## Balance and build notes

Keep a rock-paper-scissors loop between archetypes so every fighter has matchups they like and fear; no one should beat everything.

| Archetype | Fighters | Usually beats | Usually struggles vs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rushdown / fast | Cecilia, Juel, Flan | Zoners and setplay before setup (Lila, Sara, Ronald) | Bruiser armor and big trades (Silica), Noah's trapped corner |
| Bruiser / armor | Silica, Flan | Rushdown that mashes or relies on small hits (Juel, Cecilia) | Zoners who never let her in (Ronald, Lila) |
| Zoners | Lila, Ronald, Leticia | Slow fighters (Silica, Flan) | Fast rushdown that gets past shots (Juel, Cecilia) |
| Setplay | Sara, Noah, Eustace, Soran | Defensive fighters once set up (Ronald, Silica) | Rushdown that hits them during setup |

**Health tiers (suggested, % of a baseline):** Low 90% (Juel, Lila), Low-medium 95% (Cecilia, Sara), Medium 100% (Leticia, Ronald, Soran, Eustace, Noah), Medium-high 105% (Flan), High 115% (Silica).

**Uniqueness rule:** each fighter owns ONE signature mechanic and no one else gets it. If two characters start sharing a tool (for example, a second character with traps), give it a different cost or range.

**Suggested build order** (simplest systems first, each one adds a new engine piece):

1. Cecilia (done): rekkas, stance.
2. Silica: armor and command grab.
3. Leticia: projectiles and a resource (ammo).
4. Soran: one spawned object on screen (the sigil).
5. Ronald and Lila: charge inputs, opponent status marks.
6. Flan and Juel: hold-to-charge buttons, timed buffs, teleports.
7. Sara: several objects with timers.
8. Eustace: a second, controllable body.
9. Noah: objects plus recoverable health costs (the most systems).
