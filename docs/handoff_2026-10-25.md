# Handoff — Oct 25 2026 (landing on crouch, EX pause, Leticia's beams, sprite sizes, group lobbies, Cecilia's render)

**Build first:** C# changed (engine + netcode). Open the project in Godot and press **Build** (hammer, top right) before
playing, or the game runs the old code (and the online lobby can't find `OnlineRoom`'s new functions).

## 1. Mashing down looked like landing from a jump
Standing up from a crouch played the jump **landing** state (pose, dust, landing sound). Every fighter now has its own
**Crouch_Exit** state (`actors/<fighter>/States/<TAG>_CrouchExit.tres`, its `8_<TAG>_CrouchExit` animation, no dust, no
sound), used by the Uncrouch move and the crouch's exit; states 11-13 are named Landing again.
Tool: `tools/sprite_port/crouch_exit.py`. Docs: `docs/game_systems.md` → Movement.

## 2. EX moves and supers pause the fight
When an EX move (2 bars) or a super (3 bars) starts, the whole fight **stops** (EX 30 frames, super 40): nothing moves,
the clock stops, the **camera zooms in** on the fighter who did it, the stage goes dark and the opponent turns dark.
Then it eases back out and the flash / cut-in carry on. Saved in the match state (rollback-safe: sync tests clean).
Settings: GameManager node `NetsuPauseExFrames` / `NetsuPauseSuperFrames`, Camera3D node `NetsuZoom...`,
`netsu_super_flash.gd` `pause_ex_dim` / `pause_super_dim` / `pause_out`. Docs: `docs/game_systems.md` → EX Crafts.

## 3. Leticia's guns

## 4. Fighters growing / shrinking between frames
New tool `tools/sprite_port/sizefix.py`: compares every pair of drawings a fighter uses and measures the face 3 ways,
and fixes a picture's scale only where the measures agree. Fixed: **Flan** 26 pictures (her S-Craft / 236 charge
frames were ~20-25 % too big, j.B ~20 % too small, and a dozen 5-10 % ones), **Leticia** 22 (5-16 %: j.B, dashes,
blocks, 2B, DP, S2...), **Cecilia** 1 (the charge pose of her Lightning Dash 214, also used by Brave Cancel / Burst:
6.5 % too big). Their manifests (`tools/sprite_port/manifests/*.json`,
old values kept in `scale_note`), the rebuilt sprites (`actors/<fighter>/Sprites/v2/`) and animations / hitboxes.

## 5. Group online lobbies: king of the hill + spectators
Up to **8 players** per lobby. Everyone stands in one line; the first two fight (king vs challenger), the **winner
stays**, the **loser goes to the back**; everyone else **watches live** (it opens by itself; B = back to the lobby,
A in the lobby = watch again; someone joining mid-fight watches from the start, fast-forwarded). S = sit out / back
in line. 60 s to pick. Names: online menu → NAME. Leaving mid-fight loses the match; the host leaving closes the lobby.
- New: `game/autoloads/online_room.gd` (autoload **OnlineRoom**, added to `project.godot`: the rules), rewritten room
  screen `scenes/online/online_lobby.gd`, `engine/netcode/NetsuOnline.cs` (several players, relaying fights between two
  non-hosts, the spectator stream), `PleaseResyncManager.cs` (player 1 streams the settled frames, spectators play them
  back), `GameManager.NetsuMatchResult()`, `MatchSetup.start_online_room()`, names on the VS / win screens.
- Protocol version is now `netsu-vs-online-2`: everyone needs this build.
- Tested with 3 and 4 copies of the game (KOTH rotation over 5 matches, host as spectator, matches between two
  non-hosts, late joiner, sit out, unwatch / rewatch, leaving mid-fight, lag + loss on the streaming player): players and
  every spectator ended every match with identical health and gauges. Docs: `docs/online.md`.
