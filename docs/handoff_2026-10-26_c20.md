## Fix: locked in the down state after Cecilia's counter super
c18's "stay down until the super's ending pose is over" (`NetsuHeldDownBySuper`) met Cecilia's counter super, which
holds its sheathe pose until the opponent is out of hit reaction (`NetsuOpponentHitReactionCondition` + GoToFrame):
each waited for the other. Now the hold never applies when the attacker's current state checks
`NetsuOpponentHitReactionCondition`, nor once the attacker's state is past its animation's end, and never for more
than `NetsuSuperHoldMax` (150) frames past the usual get-up time.

## OTG window: 180 frames, shrinking with proration (shown on the juggle meter)
- `GameManager.NetsuOtgWindowFrames` 45 → 180 (range up to 300). `SakugaActor.NetsuOtgWindow()` now scales across the
  whole proration range: 180 × (scaling − 15) / 85, so full at 100% scaling, half at ~57%, none at 15%. OTG hits
  scale the combo, so the window shrinks as you hit them on the floor.
- They lie until the window is over (+6 frames, `NetsuDownLimit()`, at least `NetsuDownTotalFrames` = 50), then get
  up (invincible). The lying animation holds near its end until then.
- Juggle meter (`netsu_hud.gd`, `MatchSetup.netsu_juggle_hud` now takes `lying, otg_left, otg_max`): while they lie,
  the top bar shows the OTG window left in cyan with the label **OTG**; when it runs out, **DOWN**. Colour export:
  `juggle_otg`.
- Note: a knockdown early in a combo now keeps them down up to ~3 s. Lower `NetsuOtgWindowFrames` if that's too long.
