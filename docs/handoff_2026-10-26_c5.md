# Handoff — Oct 26 (c5): landing recovery, block advantage, jump cancels, follow-up hits, Cecilia j.C

Branch: TamaDeez. **C# changed, so Build before playing.** Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c5.zip`.

## Landing recovery (all characters)
- A jump with no attack lands with **0** frames of recovery.
- A jump where she used an air normal lands with **2 / 3 / 4** frames (j.A / j.B / j.C). The strongest button used that jump counts, and a held-button air move counts as C.
- During those frames she can't attack or block. A button pressed during them stays buffered and comes out right after.
- Code: `SakugaActor.PreTick` (NetsuLandLag / NetsuAirAttackLag / NetsuWasAir, saved for rollback as keys 22–24) and `StanceManager.ExecuteMove`, `ProcessMoveBuffer` and `GetBlockState`.

## Frame advantage on block (all characters)
Blockstun is now set from the attacker's move, so a ground-blocked move leaves the attacker at:

| | A (light) | B (medium) | C (hard) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normals (5X, 2X) | −4 | −5 | −8 |
| Specials | −4 | −6 | −6 |

- The button strength is read from the move's input (A=light, B=medium, C=hard).
- These keep their own blockstun: EX moves, supers, 623 (DP) moves, air moves, projectiles, assists and two-button moves.
- On a multi-hit move, each hit sets the blockstun so the last one decides the advantage.
- Code: `StanceManager.NetsuBlockAdvantage` / `NetsuStrength`, used at the start of `SakugaActor.BlockHit`.

## Jump cancels
- **5A and 5B:** jump-cancel on hit or block.
- **2B:** jump-cancel on hit only.
- 5C, 2C and 2A are unchanged.
- Any ground jump works, including super jump. Dash jump doesn't.
- Code: `StanceManager.NetsuJumpCancel` (called from `CheckMoveCancel`).

## Specials whiffing on hit
- **Cause:** when a hit sent the attacker into a follow-up state (a special's second part, a counter's slash), the engine marked the opponent as "already hit" right after the switch. The follow-up's first hitbox then passed through them.
- **Fix:** if the hit switched or restarted the state, the opponent is no longer marked (`SakugaActor.HitConfirm`). This helps every character.
