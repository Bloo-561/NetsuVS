# Handoff — Oct 26 (c8): counter super explosion lands on the opponent

**C# changed (one action), so Build.** Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c8.zip`.

## Bug
`NetsuPlaceAction.FaceBack` turned the fighter around using `Body.IsLeftSide`. The game recomputes that every frame from the fighters' positions. Cecilia's counter super places her past the opponent first and turns her a few frames later. By then IsLeftSide was already updated, so "turn around" made her face **away** from the opponent. After the last slash she faced the wrong way, and the sheathe explosion (drawn and hit-boxed 1.2 m in front of her) went off about 2.4 m from the opponent, off screen.

## Fix
- **Engine:** `NetsuPlaceAction` FaceBack now turns from her current facing (`PlayerSide`). Only the counter super uses FaceBack.
- **`92_CEC_S1_hit.tres`:**
  - She turns on the frame she reaches the far side (frames 26 / 36 / 46 / 56).
  - The extra place on frames 27 / 37 / 47 / 57 is removed; with the corrected turn it would have teleported her back.
  - New: on frame 75 she's placed 1.2 m in front of the opponent before the sheathe, so the explosion (frame 84) and its hitbox are always on them.
