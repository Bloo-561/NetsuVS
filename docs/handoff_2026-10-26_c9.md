# Handoff — Oct 26 (c9): counter super, the opponent stays up for the explosion

Data only (no Build needed for this batch). Old file is in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c9.zip`.

## Bug
The four slashes put the opponent in the crumple reaction. Crumple plays out and drops them into a knockdown before the sheathe explosion (frame 84). Supers can't hit a lying opponent, so the explosion whiffed.

## Fix
`92_CEC_S1_hit.tres`: on frame 54, right after the last slash's hit window, the opponent is frozen for 32 frames (`NetsuFreezeOpponentAction`). They hang in their stagger pose through the sheathe, and the explosion at frame 84 hits and launches them.
