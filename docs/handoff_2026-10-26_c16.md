# Handoff — Oct 26 (c16): 6C upper-body invuln, easy super jump, Flan DP / j236, Overdrive rule, Leticia 214B / j236

**C# changed (InputManager, StanceManager), so Build.** Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c16.zip`.

## 6C anti-air: upper-body invulnerability (everyone)
- During 6C's startup and active frames, each fighter's hurtbox above 55% of her standing height is removed. Boxes that cross the line are clipped to it. Air attacks have nothing up top to hit; ground attacks (which reach the floor) still hit.
- Cut lines (drawn units; 55% of the idle hurtbox top): Cecilia 7340, Flan 7290, Leticia 8030, Noah 8030.
- Files: `CEC_Hitbox_6C_0/1`, Flan / Noah `8_Hitbox_6C_0/1/2`, `LET_Hitbox_6C_0/1`.

## Super jump: just 2 then 8 (`InputManager.NetsuSuperJumpMotion`)
- **Before:** Mahou's matcher wanted down, then **neutral**, then up as the last three input changes, each at most 6 frames long. Holding down a bit, going 2 → 8 directly, or rolling through 1 / 3 failed.
- **Now:** any down (1 / 2 / 3) held within `NetsuSuperJumpWindow` (14) frames, then a fresh up. 7 / 8 / 9 still decides back / neutral / forward.
- Jump cancels (`StanceManager.NetsuJumpCancel`) now scan the move list from the end, so a 2~8 super jump wins there too.

## Overdrive: never out of a super
- `StanceManager.NetsuBurstOrOverdriveState`: Overdrive is refused while a super or its follow-up states run (`NetsuSuperActive`, or a current move costing 6000+).
- Still allowed from neutral, normals, specials / EX moves and block.

  - Simulated: Flan is 3000–8800 units above them through the Falling Star window (frames 22–34).
