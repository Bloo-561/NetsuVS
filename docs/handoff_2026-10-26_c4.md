## 3-bar supers hit harder
Every hit of a 3-bar super does **150%** of its hitboxes' damage (chip on block too): Cecilia's Thunder Plunge and
counter super, Flan's Radiant Breaker and Big Bang Crash, Leticia's Stardrive Cannon and Crimson Waltz, Noah's Raging
Storm and God Press. It counts from the moment the super starts, through its follow-up states (Radiant Breaker's burst,
the counter super's slash, Big Bang Crash's sky slam...) and anything the fighter spawns during it, until she's back
out of her attack states. Combo scaling still applies. Assists never get it.
One number: `SakugaActor.NetsuSuperDamagePercent`. The flag is `NetsuSuperActive` (saved for rollback,
`SakugaActorState` key 21), set in `StanceManager.ExecuteMove`.

## Health a bit lower (still above the original)
Cecilia / Leticia / Noah 14000 → **12500** (were 10000 originally), Flan 15400 → **13750** (was 11000).
