**C# changed (SakugaActor, AnimationViewer, GameManager, SakugaActorState), so Build.** GDScript changed (netsu_hud.gd, match_setup.gd). Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c15.zip`.

## Juggle system (BlazBlue / Under Night style)
- **Combo time:** `SakugaActor.NetsuComboFrames` counts how long the combo on a fighter has lasted (frames in a hit reaction, hitstop not counted). It's reset when the combo ends. It counts every hit, so assists' hits use up the juggle too.
- **Juggle decay:** every hit's hitstun (the time before the victim can tech) loses 1 frame per `NetsuJuggleDecayEvery` (6) frames past `NetsuJuggleGraceFrames` (150 = 2.5 s), up to `NetsuJuggleMaxDecay` (45). Air hits can go down to 1 frame (they tech out); ground hits keep at least 8.
  - This **replaces** Mahou's per-hit decay (−1 per hit after the 8th hit). Gravity decay is kept.
- **Combo extenders:** specials +`NetsuSpecialUntechBonus` (3) frames, EX moves +`NetsuExUntechBonus` (8). EX moves and supers ignore `NetsuExDecayIgnorePercent` (50%) of the decay. Assists get no bonus.
- **Tuning:** all of these are exports on the GameManager node (`scenes/battle/battle.tscn`).
- **Juggle meter** (`netsu_hud.gd` `_draw_juggle`, Inspector group *Juggle Meter*): under the attacker's combo counter.

  | Part | Shows |
  |---|---|
  | Top bar | Hitstun left, out of what the last hit gave (time before they can tech) |
  | Thin bar | Juggle left. It shrinks as the combo gets long, and the colour goes green → yellow → red. |
  | TECH! | They can tech now; another hit makes the combo invalid |
  | AIR COUNTER | Purple: no tech until they land |
  | INVALID | The combo has gone invalid |
  | DOWN | Hard knockdown |

  Data comes from `SakugaActor.NetsuJuggleHud` → `MatchSetup.netsu_juggle_hud` / `juggle_hud`. It replaces the old thin stun line.

## Counter hits
- **Counter / punish counter:** the victim flashes **red** for 18 frames (`NetsuCounterColor`).
- **Air counter hit** (the victim was airborne): the victim pulses **purple** and **can't tech until touching the ground**. `NetsuAirCounterLock` keeps the hitstun from running out while they're airborne.
- The flashes use the tech flash's overlay, which now takes a colour (`AnimationViewer.SetWhite(amount, color)`).
- New rollback keys 26–29: NetsuComboFrames, NetsuUntechMax, NetsuAirCounterLock, NetsuCounterFlashAt.

## HP bars
- Anchored at the **timer** end, emptying from the portrait side (BlazBlue style): `netsu_hud.gd` `_draw_health`.
