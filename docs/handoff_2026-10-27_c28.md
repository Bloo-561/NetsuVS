## The same pushback anywhere on screen
- Before: midscreen the opponent slid back by the hit's knockback (normals at 60%, `NetsuNormalPushbackPercent`).
  In the corner the opponent can't move, so the attacker was pushed instead by the hitbox's flat corner push
  (`SelfPushbackForce` 30000 for 8 frames) at full strength, whatever the move: about 0.58 m, about twice a light
  normal's midscreen push (5A ~0.3 m).
- Now (`SakugaActor.HitPushback`): against the wall the attacker slides back by exactly the push the opponent got
  (same speed, same frames, same 60% for normals), on hit and on block. The gap after any hit or block is the same
  midscreen and in the corner.
- Unchanged: a hitbox with its corner push off (`AllowSelfPushback` false, or `SelfPushbackForce` / `Duration` 0)
  pushes nothing; no pushback when the attacker is in the air; OTG hits push nothing; projectiles keep their old
  corner push (it only moves the projectile).

## Testing checklist
1. 5A, 2B, 5C on block midscreen, then the same in the corner: you end up the same distance apart.
2. A light-normal chain in the corner (5A 5A 2A...) stays as close as it does midscreen.
3. Jump-in in the corner: no extra push while in the air (as before).
