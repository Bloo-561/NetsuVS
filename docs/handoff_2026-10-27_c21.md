Flux Dive A / B spiral (`41_Hitbox_FluxDive_A_3`, `42_Hitbox_FluxDive_B_5`) and the Flux Storm spiral
(`43_Hitbox_FluxStorm_7`) now knock down. The c19 version used Air_Strong (reaction 5), which only turns into a

recovered on their feet. Now:
- air and ground hits use Hit_LaunchDown (reaction 9), which goes to Hit_Knockdown the moment they touch the floor
  (no bounce: BounceTime 0); a standing opponent hit by the spiral is knocked down too (knockback (-2000, -20000));
- hitstun 40, KNOCKDOWN type (soft: they can tech / quick-rise as usual, not a hard knockdown).
