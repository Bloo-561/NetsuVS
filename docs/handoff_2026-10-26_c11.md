# Handoff — Oct 26 (c11): 236C float fix, 214C crumples in place

**C# changed, so Build.** Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c11.zip`.

## Opponent stuck in the air after Cecilia's 236C
- **Cause:** `PushActor` sets the victim's body gravity to the hit's knockback gravity. The 236C finisher (`42_Hitbox_236C_2`) launches from the ground (knockback Y 45000) but only set gravity for air hits. `GroundHitKnockbackGravity` defaults to 0, so they rose and stayed up.
- **Data fix:** `42_Hitbox_236C_2` now has `GroundHitKnockbackGravity = 200000`.
- **Engine safety net** (`SakugaActor.HitDamage`, every character): a hit with gravity 0 that sends the victim up, or lands while they're in the air, now uses `NetsuLaunchGravity` (200000). Gravity 1 (deliberately holding them up, as in the 214C rush) is untouched.

## 214C finishers: crumple in place → hard knockdown
- **Hitboxes:** `57_Hitbox_DashRush_9` (the rush finisher) and `120_Hitbox_214C_DashSlash_2` (the late dash hit when the rush doesn't start).
  - Ground, crouch and air reactions are all crumple (6).
  - Knockback (0, 0), HitstunType HARD_KNOCKDOWN, hitstun 60.
  - Crumple's animation ends into Hit_Down. With a hard knockdown there's no tech.
- **`97_CEC_RUSH.tres`:** on a hit from frame 78 on, `NetsuRestandAction` brings an airborne opponent straight down, so the crumple plays on the floor where they were.
