## Assists: not in the air
S does nothing while airborne, **except in Overdrive** (`NetsuAssist.CanCall`).

## Leticia: smaller
New `NetsuScale` on each fighter's data (per mille, 1000 = as drawn). Leticia's is **940**: she was drawn ~10% taller
than Flan, now ~4% taller. Her sprites and her attack / throw boxes shrink with it around her feet
(`SakugaActor.Render`, `PhysicsBody.NetsuBox`); her hurtboxes keep the universal height. Change it in
`actors/leticia/0_LET_Data.tres`. Her projectiles and their spawn points are unchanged.

## Leticia: charged j.C
Keep **C held** through j.C's startup (still held on frame 8): she holds the pose 18 frames with cyan energy swirling
at her raised gun (falling slowly), then a bigger slash (boxes x1.25, 900 damage). **On hit it slams them down into a
ground bounce.** Tap C = the normal j.C. New state 110 (`States/110_LET_j5C_charged.tres`), hitbox
`Hitboxes/15_Hitbox_j_5C_charged.tres`; the hold check is in `States/71_LET_J5C.tres`.

## Cecilia
- **Counter super hits now.** It had no hit: the counter catch marks the attacker as "already hit", and the counter
  state never cleared that, so the slash box couldn't connect. Fixed (ResetHit at frames 1 and 5).
- **Slower:** each crossing takes 10 frames instead of 7 (the whole move is ~13 frames longer).
- **Lightning cuts:** each crossing now shows her 236 lightning cut on the opponent (horizontal on crossings 1 and 3,
  diagonal on 2 and 4).
- **214C (EX Dash Slash) in the corner:** the last hit is now a wide blast on both sides of her
  (`Hitboxes/57_Hitbox_DashRush_9.tres`), so it hits even when she couldn't get past them in the corner.
