## Every ground normal cancels into 6C
5A, 5B, 5C, 2A, 2B, 2C (and 4A for Cecilia and Leticia) can cancel into the 6C launcher on hit or on block, like any
other chain. Whether it **combos** is the frame data's call: 6C's startup against the normal's hitstun (a counter hit adds
12 frames of hitstun). On block it's a pressure option, not a combo.

| Fighter | 6C startup | Combos into 6C | Only on counter hit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cecilia | 15 | 2B, 5B, 5C | 5A, 2A, 4A |
| Leticia | 15 | 2B, 5B, 5C | 5A, 2A, 4A |
| Flan | 17 | 5B, 5C | 5A, 2A, 2B |
| Noah | 17 | 5B, 5C | 5A, 2A, 2B |
2C is a sweep (knockdown): 6C after it doesn't combo, but 2C can still be cancelled into it.

Then, as before: on hit press 8 (or 9 / 7) to jump after them into an air combo.

### What changed for this
- **Cecilia's and Leticia's 6C are slower:** 15 frames of startup (were 11 and 10), so their lights don't combo into it
  without a counter hit (like Flan and Noah). The animation holds the wind-up a little longer.
- **6C reaches further:** every 6C's attack box was only at the fist / blade tip above the head (an anti-air). After a
  normal pushed them back, 6C whiffed. It now also covers the body in front, out to about 1 m (`BOX_6C` in
  `tools/sprite_port/launchers.py`; still hits jumpers above).
- **Cancel order:** in a cancel list 6C is checked after the specials, so 5A > 236C still comes out as 236C, not 6C
  (`fdk.add_cancels` now follows each move's `NetsuCheckOrder`).
- The moves scripts (`moves_<fighter>.py`) add the normal → 6C cancels again whenever they rewrite the normals.
- Pause-menu move list: 6C says "any normal cancels into it".
- Frame data sheets rebuilt (6C rows).
