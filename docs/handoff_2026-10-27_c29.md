## Corner pushback, much less
- `GameManager.NetsuCornerPushbackPercent` = **25**: against the wall the attacker slides back only 25% of the push
  the opponent would have taken midscreen (c28 was 100%). A 5A now moves you ~6 cm in the corner instead of ~30 cm.
  0 = none; 100 = same as midscreen. Inspector setting on the GameManager node.

## Projectiles always go the way you faced when you used the move
- Cause: a spawned projectile copied the shooter's *side of the opponent* (updated every frame from positions), not
  its *facing*. After a cross-up or an air hit the two differ until the fighter turns, so Shade Wave (and every other
  projectile) could fly backwards. `ObjectPool.SpawnObjectCommon` now uses the shooter's facing (`PlayerSide`).
- Mary's 6S: she already faced the caller, but read the facing when she appears (after the call delay), not when S
  was pressed. `NetsuAssist` now saves the fighter's facing at the call (`PendingSide`, rollback key 15) and uses it.
