# Handoff — Oct 26 (c6): counter super hits, no 5C jump cancel, assist directions

Branch: TamaDeez. **C# changed, so Build before playing.** Old files are in `_backup_stage_select/pre_push_oct26_c6.zip`.

## Engine bug: hits ignored while boxes overlap (the counter super cause)
- **File:** `Collision/PhysicsWorld.cs` (`HitboxesCheck`).
- **Bug:** when one box pair between two fighters couldn't hit (that attack had already hit), the engine stopped checking the whole fighter pair.
- **Effect:** after Cecilia's counter, the opponent is frozen with their used-up attack still overlapping her. So none of her slashes were ever checked. The same thing could make other hits whiff during trades and overlaps.
- **Fix:** it now skips only that box pair.

## Cecilia counter super (states 85 → 92)
Sequence:
1. Counter stance catches a hit.
2. Super flash (20 frames).
3. Slow draw: op02 pose, then op03 at frame 9. The countered opponent stays frozen through it (`NetsuFreezeOpponentAction`).
4. Four slash-throughs at frames 18, 28, 38 and 48. Each one has a ResetHit, so every pass hits: 700 damage, unblockable, crumple.
5. Sheath at frame 76: 1800 damage, launches the opponent up into a knockdown (`47_Hitbox_Sakura_Attack_4.tres`). The restand on hit is limited to frames 0–75 so it doesn't cancel the launch.
6. Frame 120 is held (`GoToFrame` 119) while the opponent is still in a hit reaction (`NetsuOpponentHitReactionCondition`). She can't move until they have recovered.

Damage is listed before the 150% super bonus and combo scaling. Tune it in `Hitboxes/47_Hitbox_Sakura_Attack_1.tres` (slashes) and `_4.tres` (sheath).

## No jump cancel on 5C (or any other normal except 5A / 5B / 2B)
- Cecilia's 5C (and her 5B) listed the jumps in their cancel lists.
- `StanceManager.NetsuJumpCancelBlocked` now enforces one rule for everyone, whatever the data says:
  - 5A and 5B jump-cancel on hit or block;
  - 2B jump-cancels on hit only;
  - every other normal can't jump-cancel.

## Assist directions (`NetsuAssist.ReadInput`)
- **6S:** S with any left/right in it (7, 4, 1, 9, 6, 3).
- **5S:** S with no direction.
- **8 + S or 2 + S:** calls 5S only during Overdrive. Outside Overdrive it calls nothing.
