## Untech time (`GameManager`)
- `NetsuAirUntechBonus` = 8: every hit on an opponent in the air gets 8 more frames before they can tech.
- Time decay still shortens it the longer the combo lasts: grace 150 -> 180 frames, max 45 -> 55 frames.
- **Repeat proration** (new): a move already used earlier in this combo gives less untech time, per earlier use
  (counted up to 3): normals `NetsuRepeatNormalUntech` = 3 frames, specials / EX / supers `NetsuRepeatSpecialUntech`
  = 7 frames. One use of a multi-hit move counts once; a projectile counts as the move that threw it; assists don't
  count. Air hits only (ground hitstun isn't touched). Forgotten when the combo ends.
  Example: 5C, j.C, 5C again: the 2nd 5C has 3 frames less; a 2nd 236A in a juggle has 7 less, a 3rd 14 less.

## OTG
- `NetsuOtgWindowFrames` 100 -> **90**. Still scaled by the combo's proration, but only once, when they land; after
  that it's fixed (OTG hits' own damage scaling no longer shrinks it).
- Each OTG hit takes a little off by button: A 4, B 7, C 10 frames (`NetsuOtgCostA/B/C`; two-button / no-button
  moves cost the B amount). The OTG bar drops by that much. They still lie 45 frames (DOWN) after it before getting up.
