## Flux Dive (j.236A / B) and Flux Storm (j.236C): the spiral drags them down
- The spiral (board twirl, `fb_5` / `fb_6`) now has an attack box for its whole length, not just its first 6 frames:
  `84_LET_FA_AnimData` / `85_LET_FB_AnimData` keep `41_Hitbox_FluxDive_A_3` / `42_Hitbox_FluxDive_B_5` active
  until the end (the old recovery boxes A_4 / B_6 are no longer used).
- The spiral box hits every 8 frames (`ResetHitAction` events in states 84 / 85 / 92) for 200 damage (250 on Flux
  Storm), hitstop 6, Air_Strong with KNOCKDOWN hitstun 20 (no ground bounce any more).
- On hit she carries them with her (`NetsuHoldOpponentAction` Offset (4500, -3000), frames 9 to the end), so they
  reach the floor together with her. They're in a normal knockdown (not hard), so they can tech right after landing.
- **Flux Storm** had no spiral: after its third flip it now goes into the same spiral dive (frames 44+, X 27500 /
  Y -37500, the rest of the animation is the spiral loop; duration 58 → 140). The third flip
  (`43_Hitbox_FluxStorm_5`) no longer spikes; it pops them like the first two, and the spiral finishes the move. New
  box `43_Hitbox_FluxStorm_7` (spiral). Gravity only applies before the dive (frames 1-43). She no longer gets the free
  fall with air control at the end: she spirals until she lands.
